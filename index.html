Short-form gaming clips

theSpoils Clips

theSpoils Clips turns live gaming streams into vertical short clips for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels and X. Every clip credits the streamer and links back to their channel, and we only clip creators who allow it or who run an official clipping campaign.

Our tool ("theSpoils Clipper") uploads our own finished clips to our own YouTube channel, @theSpoilsClips, through the YouTube Data API. It is used only by the theSpoils team.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Who uses the app

theSpoils Clipper is an internal tool. It is not offered to the public, and it signs in only with the theSpoils team's own Google account.

What we access through YouTube API Services

  • youtube.upload: to upload our own finished clips to our own channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to confirm which of our channels we are uploading to and check upload status.

We do not access, collect or store data about other YouTube users or viewers.

How data is stored

The Google sign-in token is stored only on our own computer, in a file readable only by our account. Video metadata (titles, descriptions, tags) is written by us. We do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also removes our stored access.
